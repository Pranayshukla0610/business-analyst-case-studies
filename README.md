# business-analyst-case-studies
This repository contains structured business analyst case studies focused on:

- Business problem solving
- KPI analysis
- Requirement understanding
- Market and operational analysis
- Process improvement strategies
- Business frameworks
- Data-driven decision making

The case studies are designed to demonstrate analytical thinking, business acumen, strategic reasoning, and structured problem-solving approaches commonly expected in Business Analyst interviews and real-world scenarios.

## Topics Covered

- Sales Forecasting
- Customer Retention Analysis
- KPI Framework Design
- Revenue & Growth Analysis
- Operational Optimization
- Marketplace Analytics
- Business Strategy Cases
- Product & User Analytics
- Stakeholder-Centric Problem Solving

## Purpose

This repository is intended for:
- Interview preparation
- Business analysis learning
- Case study practice
- Portfolio building
- Structured analytical thinking
